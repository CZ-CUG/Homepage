I am a Professor at the **School of Automation, China University of Geosciences (Wuhan)**. My research focuses on multi-agent cooperative systems, resilient control theory, distributed sensing coverage, active disturbance rejection control (ADRC), and human-robot/social motor coordination.

---

## 🔬 Research Focus & Areas

- **Multi-Agent Cooperative & Coverage Control:** Distributed algorithms, barrier/sectorial coverage in non-convex environments, conformal mapping, load balancing, and UAV system scheduling.
- **Active Disturbance Rejection & Flight Simulation Systems:** ADRC parameter tuning, tracking differentiators (TD), extended state observers (ESO), and pressure/temperature control in high-altitude environmental test facilities.
- **Resilient Systems & Cyber-Physical Security:** Control Barrier Functions (CBFs), safety-critical cooperative optimal control, and game-theoretic resilience in complex networks.
- **Social Motor Coordination & Human-Robot Interaction:** Interpersonal motor coordination, mirror games, and evolutionary game theory in networked systems.
